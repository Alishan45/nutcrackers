## 1. Strategy & Methodology

Nutcrackers targets high-intent commercial and transactional queries where clients are actively seeking agency partners, custom engineering, or enterprise automation. Rather than pursuing overly broad vanity keywords (e.g. "AI"), our architecture targets **two parallel tracks**:

1. **Commercial Service Landing Queries:** Focused on direct procurement (e.g., "Web development company in Pakistan", "AI automation services Islamabad", "Custom mobile app development agency").
2. **Informational Problem-Solving Queries:** Focused on CTOs, founders, and marketing directors seeking technical guidance (e.g., "How to choose a web development company", "AI chatbots vs traditional customer support").
